# How do I see which theme comes up most?

1. Open **Surveys**. On **Load**, press **Try the sample** (or **Open a data file** for your own), read the privacy check and confirm it, and say which column holds the answers.
2. On **Themes**, pick a ready-made set or type your own.
3. On **Code**, keep **Suggest codes for me to check**. Press **Quick preview** first: it tries 50 answers and writes nothing to your project. Then press **Run the analysis**.
4. On **Explore**, open **Analysis**, then **How often**. The longest bar is the theme that comes up most. Click a bar to read the answers behind it.

**Good to know.** The suggestions are a first draft. Look at the answers marked **Needs your review** before you quote a number.
