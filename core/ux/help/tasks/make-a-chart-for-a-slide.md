# How do I make a chart for a slide?

1. Open **Charts**. Press **Open a CSV or Excel file**. To practise, press **Try the sample survey**.
2. Pick the question to chart. QualSafe chooses a chart that fits.
3. On **Adjust**, filter the choices, rename them, and reorder them. Add a title.
4. On **Save**, press **Copy picture**. Paste the picture into PowerPoint or Word. Or press **Save picture** and choose PNG at 300 dots per inch.

**Good to know.** Each bar has a label with the percent and the counts. An example is 26% (63/240). Press **Save table** to keep the numbers as a spreadsheet.
