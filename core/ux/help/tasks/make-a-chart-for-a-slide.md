# How do I make a chart for a slide?

1. Open **Charts**. Press **Open a CSV or Excel file**, or **Try the sample survey** to practise.
2. Pick the question to chart. QualSafe chooses a chart that fits.
3. On **Adjust**, filter, rename and reorder the choices, and add a title.
4. On **Save**, press **Copy picture** and paste it into PowerPoint or Word, or press **Save picture** and choose PNG at 300 dots per inch.

**Good to know.** Each bar is labelled with the percent and the counts, for example 26% (63/240). Press **Save table** to keep the numbers as a spreadsheet.
