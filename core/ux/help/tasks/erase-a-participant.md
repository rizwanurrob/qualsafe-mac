# How do I erase a participant?

1. Open **Settings**, then **Project and backups**.
2. Press **Erase this participant**, choose the person, and type ERASE to confirm.
3. Decide whether to remove the project's backups too. Leave that on if the backups hold the same person.

**Good to know.** Erasing removes the person's responses from the project file itself, not just from the screen. It cannot be undone.
