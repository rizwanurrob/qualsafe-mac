# How do I erase a participant?

1. Open **Settings**. Then open **Project and backups**.
2. Press **Erase this participant**. Choose the person. Type ERASE to confirm.
3. Decide if you also remove the backups of the project. Leave this option on if the backups hold the same person.

**Good to know.** The erase removes the responses of the person from the project file. It does not only hide them on the screen. You cannot undo it.
