# Surveys, step 1: Load your answers

**What this step is for.** QualSafe needs your survey answers in a table: one row for each person (or each answer) and a column that holds the written text.

**What to do**

1. Press **Open a data file** and choose your CSV or Excel file. To practise first, press **Use the sample answers**: it is made-up data about a first year at university, 40 answers. (For a finished example to read, use Try the sample survey on Home.)
2. Say which column holds the written answers. Everything else is optional.
3. Read the privacy check. It looks for names, emails and phone numbers in the text. You confirm it every time you open a file; QualSafe never remembers that you did.

**Good to know**

- Your file is read, never changed.
- If each row has several question columns, open **More options** and say so. QualSafe then treats each answer as its own item, still linked to the person who gave it.
- Nothing leaves your computer. This step does not use the internet.
