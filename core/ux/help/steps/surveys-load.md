# Surveys, step 1: Load your answers

**What this step is for.** QualSafe needs your survey answers in a table. Each row is one person or one answer. One column holds the written text.

**What to do**

1. Press **Open a data file**. Choose your CSV or Excel file.
2. To practise first, press **Use the sample answers**. The sample uses made-up data. It has 40 answers about a first year at university.
3. Select the column that holds the written answers. All other choices are optional.
4. Read the privacy check. It looks for names, emails, and phone numbers in the text.
5. Confirm the privacy check. You must confirm it each time you open a file. QualSafe never remembers that you confirmed it.

**Good to know**

- QualSafe reads your file. It does not change your file.
- Your file can have several question columns in each row. If it does, open **More options** and say so. QualSafe then treats each answer as its own item. Each item stays linked to the person who gave it.
- Nothing leaves your computer. This step does not use the internet.
- For a finished example, use Try the sample survey on Home.
