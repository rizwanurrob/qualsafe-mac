# Charts, step 4: Save

**What this step is for.** Get the chart out of QualSafe, or keep it for later.

**What to do**

- **Save picture** writes a PNG (choose 150 or 300 dots per inch for slides or print), SVG or PDF. SVG stays sharp at any size.
- **Copy picture** puts it on the clipboard to paste into PowerPoint or Word.
- **Save table** writes the numbers as a spreadsheet.
- **Keep this chart** stores the chart in the project; open it later on a file with the same columns.

**Good to know**

- A white background is the safest choice for slides.
