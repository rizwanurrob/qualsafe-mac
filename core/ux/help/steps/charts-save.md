# Charts, step 4: Save

**What this step is for.** Take the chart out of QualSafe, or keep it for later.

**What to do**

- Press **Save picture** to write a PNG, SVG, or PDF file. For a PNG, choose 150 or 300 dots per inch for slides or print. An SVG stays sharp at any size.
- Press **Copy picture** to put the chart on the clipboard. Then paste it into PowerPoint or Word.
- Press **Save table** to write the numbers as a spreadsheet.
- Press **Keep this chart** to store the chart in the project. Later, open it on a file that has the same columns.

**Good to know**

- A white background is the safest choice for slides.
