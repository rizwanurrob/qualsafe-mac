# Interviews, step 4: Explore across interviews

**What this step is for.** See patterns across people: which codes come up, in whom, and in whose words.

**What to do**

- **Analysis** counts codes by interview or by group. Click a bar to read the passages.
- **Search** finds passages by word or code.
- **Charts of themes** turns a result into a picture.
- **Model an outcome** and **Query** are for further questions; **Query** is under **More options**.

**Good to know**

- With few interviews, counts are small. Read the passages, not just the numbers.

**Next:** Report.
