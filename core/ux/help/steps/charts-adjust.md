# Charts, step 3: Adjust the chart

**What this step is for.** Make the chart show what you mean. You can filter to the people that you want. You can rename and reorder the choices. You can set colours and add a title.

**What to do**

- Press **Add filter** to keep only some rows. For example, keep only first-year students.
- Use the order and label controls. Put the choices in a sensible order. Give them clear names.
- Press **Undo** or **Redo** to step through your changes. Press **Reset chart** to start again.
- Find the colour themes under **More options**.

**Good to know**

- The table under the chart always shows the exact numbers behind the chart.

**Next:** Save.
