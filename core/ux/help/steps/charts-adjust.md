# Charts, step 3: Adjust the chart

**What this step is for.** Make the chart say what you mean: filter to the people you want, rename and reorder choices, set colours, add a title.

**What to do**

- **Add filter** keeps only some rows, for example first-year students.
- Use the order and label controls to put choices in a sensible order and wording.
- **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes. **Reset chart** starts over.
- Colour themes are under **More options**.

**Good to know**

- The table under the chart always shows the exact numbers behind it.

**Next:** Save.
