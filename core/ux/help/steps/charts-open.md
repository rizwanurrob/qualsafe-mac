# Charts, step 1: Open a spreadsheet

**What this step is for.** Make a chart from a table of numbers or choices. An example is a survey with tick-box questions and scale questions.

**What to do**

1. Press **Open a CSV or Excel file**. To see how it works, press **Use the sample spreadsheet**.
2. If the workbook has several sheets, choose the sheet that you want.

**Good to know**

- QualSafe reads your file. It does not change your file.
- If apostrophes or accents look wrong, save the file from Excel as a plain CSV. Then open it again.

**Next:** Pick a question.
