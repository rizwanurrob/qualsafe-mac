# Surveys, step 5: Make your report

**What this step is for.** Save the files you need to share or keep.

**What to do**

1. Choose the report you want. The **Methods and reliability appendix** is the one a reviewer or ethics board asks for. Others include the analysis report, the codebook report, a spreadsheet of the tables, and the audit trail.
2. Decide whether quotes from the answers may appear. The option is off unless you switch it on, and what you choose is what is written.
3. Press the main button and pick where to save.

**Good to know**

- The methods appendix says how the answers were coded and by whom, how well the coders agreed on each theme, and the limits that go with those numbers. It contains no response text.
- Respondent details in your file stay out of the files unless you include them.

**Done.** You can come back and change any earlier step; later steps will tell you if something needs running again.
