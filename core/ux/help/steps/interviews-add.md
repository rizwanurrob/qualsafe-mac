# Interviews, step 1: Add your transcripts

**What this step is for.** Bring your interview transcripts in, one file for each interview.

**What to do**

1. Press **Add transcripts from a folder** and choose the folder.
2. Read the privacy check and confirm it. QualSafe looks for names, emails and phone numbers. You confirm every time; it is never remembered.
3. For each transcript with two or more speakers, QualSafe proposes who is the interviewer and who is the participant, and shows you how it decided. Correct anything that is wrong and press **Use these roles**, or press **Skip** to leave the transcript as it is. You can do this again later with **Check who is speaking**.

**Good to know**

- Plain text and Word files work. The files are copied into your project; the originals are not changed.
- Speakers are found from labels at the start of a line, such as "Interviewer:" and "Respondent:", or "Q:" and "A:". Keep the labels in your files.
- Nothing is decided without you: the roles are only kept once you confirm them.
