# Interviews, step 1: Add your transcripts

**What this step is for.** Bring your interview transcripts into QualSafe. Use one file for each interview.

**What to do**

1. Press **Add transcripts from a folder**. Choose the folder.
2. Read the privacy check and confirm it. QualSafe looks for names, emails, and phone numbers. You must confirm each time. Your confirmation is never remembered.
3. Look at each transcript that has two or more speakers. QualSafe proposes who is the interviewer and who is the participant. It also shows how it decided. Correct each mistake. Then press **Use these roles**. To leave the transcript as it is, press **Skip**. You can do this again later with **Check who is speaking**.

**Good to know**

- Plain text files and Word files work. QualSafe copies the files into your project. It does not change the original files.
- QualSafe finds the speakers from labels at the start of a line. Examples are "Interviewer:" and "Respondent:", or "Q:" and "A:". Keep the labels in your files.
- QualSafe decides nothing without you. It keeps the roles only after you confirm them.
